# Visiting Senior Fellow Appointment Letter (Template)

Official template for appointing international distinguished professors and scholars (e.g., from Sri Lanka and partner universities) as Visiting Senior Fellows of the Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute (ASCRI).

---

## Formal Letter of Appointment

```text
SPECIFIED NONPROFIT CORPORATION OPEN CORAL NETWORK
Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute (ASCRI)
Toyama Research Lab: Yatsuo-machi, Toyama-shi, Toyama, Japan
Tokyo HQ: Tokyo, Japan
Web: https://coral-network.com  |  Email: research@coral-network.com

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OFFICIAL LETTER OF APPOINTMENT
Ref: ASCRI-APPT-2026-INTL-01
Date: October 1, 2026

To: [Full Name], Ph.D.
    Professor of Computer Science / Public Policy
    Faculty of [Faculty Name]
    University of [University Name], Sri Lanka

Dear Professor [Last Name],

On behalf of the Board of Directors of Specified Nonprofit Corporation Open Coral Network 
and the Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute (ASCRI), it is our profound honor to 
officially invite and appoint you as:

                VISITING SENIOR FELLOW
    at the Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute (ASCRI)

1. Term of Appointment:
   Effective from October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027.
   (This appointment is renewable annually upon mutual agreement of both parties).

2. Status and Academic Affiliation:
   - This is an honorary visiting research appointment.
   - You are officially authorized and encouraged to cite your affiliation as:
     "Visiting Senior Fellow, Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute, 
      Open Coral Network, Japan" in joint international publications, keynote addresses, 
      and grant applications.

3. Scope of International Academic Collaboration:
   (1) Joint Research & Co-Authorship:
       Conduct collaborative research on Civic Artificial Intelligence, Digital Democracy, 
       and Regional Commons Revitalization, submitting joint papers to international 
       refereed journals and prestigious academic symposia.
   (2) International Grant Applications:
       Serve as International Co-Principal Investigator (Co-PI) for bilateral and multilateral 
       grant initiatives, including the Toyota Foundation International Grant Program, 
       the United Nations Democracy Fund (UNDEF), and JST SICORP programs.
   (3) Academic Advisory & Mentorship:
       Provide academic mentorship and strategic guidance for young civic researchers 
       and participate as a distinguished keynote speaker at our annual academic events.

4. Research Integrity and Open Science:
   Both parties agree to uphold the highest standards of research ethics, data privacy, 
   and the FAIR principles of Open Science.

We are deeply excited about the pioneering contributions our joint Japan-Sri Lanka academic 
partnership will bring to the international community.

Sincerely and respectfully yours,

[Seal / Signature]

Naoya Kusunoki
Director, Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute (ASCRI)
Representative Director, Specified Nonprofit Corporation Open Coral Network
```
