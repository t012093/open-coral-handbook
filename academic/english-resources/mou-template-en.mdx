国際助成金（トヨタ財団国際助成、国連民主主義基金 UNDEF、JST SICORP等）において、申請要件または審査加点基準として求められる、**大学機関と本法人間で締結する包括学術協定書（MOU: Memorandum of Understanding）**の英文公式テンプレートです。

---

## Memorandum of Understanding (Official Template)

```text
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING FOR ACADEMIC AND RESEARCH COLLABORATION
BETWEEN
SPECIFIED NONPROFIT CORPORATION OPEN CORAL NETWORK (JAPAN)
AND
[NAME OF UNIVERSITY / FACULTY] (SRI LANKA)

This Memorandum of Understanding (hereinafter referred to as "MOU") is entered into on this 
[Day] day of [Month], 2026, by and between:

1. Specified Nonprofit Corporation Open Coral Network, an authorized nonprofit corporation 
   duly organized and existing under the laws of Japan, having its research branch, the 
   Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute (ASCRI), located in Toyama and Tokyo, Japan, 
   represented herein by its Representative Director, Naoya Kusunoki (hereinafter "OCN/ASCRI");

   AND

2. [Faculty / Department Name], [University Name], an institution of higher education established 
   under the laws of Sri Lanka, located at [Campus Address, Sri Lanka], represented herein by its 
   Dean / Vice-Chancellor, [Name of Signatory] (hereinafter "[University Acronym]").

OCN/ASCRI and [University Acronym] shall collectively be referred to as the "Parties" and 
individually as a "Party."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECITALS

WHEREAS, OCN/ASCRI is engaged in advanced research and social implementation in the fields of 
Civic Artificial Intelligence, Digital Democracy, and Decentralized Regional Commons;

WHEREAS, [University Acronym] possesses distinguished academic capabilities and research 
excellence in Computer Science, Public Policy, and Information Systems;

WHEREAS, both Parties desire to establish a cooperative framework for academic research, 
scholarly exchange, and joint international grant applications;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

ARTICLE 1: OBJECTIVES AND SCOPE OF COLLABORATION
The purpose of this MOU is to foster international academic cooperation in areas of mutual 
interest. Specific areas of collaboration may include:
1. Joint formulation and submission of research proposals to international funding agencies, 
   including but not limited to the Toyota Foundation International Grant Program, the United 
   Nations Democracy Fund (UNDEF), and JST SICORP.
2. Collaborative scientific research and co-authorship of peer-reviewed journal articles.
3. Reciprocal scholarly visits and guest lectures by researchers, faculty, and fellows.
4. Mutual sharing of open-source methodologies, anonymized civic datasets, and educational tools.

ARTICLE 2: IMPLEMENTATION AND SEPARATE AGREEMENTS
Specific joint projects, exchange programs, and funding allocations arising from this MOU shall 
be set forth in written Supplemental Project Agreements signed by the authorized representatives 
of both Parties prior to execution.

ARTICLE 3: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND OPEN SCIENCE
1. All preexisting intellectual property owned by either Party prior to this MOU shall remain 
   the exclusive property of that Party.
2. Scientific data, software algorithms, and publications generated jointly under this collaboration 
   shall adhere to the principles of Open Science (FAIR data principles) and be disseminated under 
   open licenses (e.g., MIT License for software and Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0 for data/papers), 
   subject to the mutual agreement of both Parties.

ARTICLE 4: RESEARCH INTEGRITY AND ETHICS
Both Parties agree to uphold the highest standards of scientific integrity, complying with 
relevant Institutional Review Board (IRB) / Ethics Review Committee (ERC) guidelines and 
personal data protection regulations in Japan and Sri Lanka.

ARTICLE 5: FINANCIAL TERMS
This MOU does not create any binding financial obligations on either Party. Each Party shall 
bear its own operational costs, unless third-party external funding is awarded for a specific 
joint project.

ARTICLE 6: DURATION, RENEWAL, AND TERMINATION
1. This MOU shall remain in full force and effect for a period of three (3) years from the date 
   of mutual execution.
2. This MOU may be renewed or amended by mutual written agreement of both Parties.
3. Either Party may terminate this MOU at any time by giving ninety (90) days written notice to 
   the other Party, without affecting ongoing projects already underway.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties hereto have caused this Memorandum of Understanding to be 
duly executed by their authorized representatives as of the date first written above.

For and on behalf of:
SPECIFIED NONPROFIT CORPORATION OPEN CORAL NETWORK

_______________________________________
Naoya Kusunoki
Representative Director, Open Coral Network
Director, Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute
Date: __________________________________


For and on behalf of:
[FACULTY / UNIVERSITY NAME, SRI LANKA]

[Title, e.g., Dean, Faculty of Science / Vice-Chancellor]
[University Name], Sri Lanka
Date: __________________________________
```
