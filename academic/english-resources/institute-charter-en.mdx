## 2. Mission Statement

The Advanced Social Challenge Research Institute (ASCRI) is a pioneering civic research institution dedicated to theoretical formulation, empirical verification, and open-source implementation in:

1. **Plurality & Collaborative Governance**: Developing decentralized institutional designs and digital democracy mechanisms for regional and global commons.
2. **Civic Artificial Intelligence**: Building ethical, human-in-the-loop AI systems that assist community consensus building using local open data.
3. **Regenerative Regional Ecosystems**: Addressing rural depopulation, vacant house revitalizations, and multi-generational community well-being through digital-physical third places.

---

## 3. Governance and Compliance

ASCRI maintains strict adherence to international research standards and Japanese Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) guidelines:

- **Institutional Review Board (IRB)**: An independent 5-member ethics board reviewing non-invasive human-subject research, data privacy, and algorithm transparency.
- **Three Research Integrity Principles**: Explicit institutional regulations for Research Ethics, Prevention of Misconduct (Zero tolerance for FFP), and Conflict of Interest (COI) Management.
- **Security Export Control**: Comprehensive compliance with Japan's Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Act (FEFTA) for international academic collaborations.
- **Open Science Policy**: Unrestricted dissemination of research data (FAIR Data principles) and peer-reviewed preprints via CERN-backed Zenodo with persistent DOIs under Creative Commons licenses.
